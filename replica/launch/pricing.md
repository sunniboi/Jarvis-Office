## Jarvis Office pricing model

**Free, forever.**

Jarvis Office is a client-side app with zero server costs. There is no
infrastructure to charge for. The model is:

| Tier | Price | Who |
|------|-------|-----|
| Personal | Free | Anyone, forever |
| Debian package | Free | Linux desktop users |
| Commercial embed | Contact owner | Businesses bundling it in a product |

No Stripe products to create. No subscription to cancel. No renewal emails.

## If a paid tier is added later

Recommended structure:

| Tier | Price | What |
|------|-------|------|
| Free | €0 | All features, local storage |
| Pro | €3/mo or €27/yr | Cloud sync (if added), priority support |
| Team | €6/seat/mo | Shared workspace (if added) |

Billing rules to follow:
- One-click cancel from the settings page
- Clear renewal email 7 days before charge
- No per-seat surprises — fixed price per workspace
- Annual = 10 months price (2 months free)
